# Changelog

## 1.2.0 · 2026-10-05

- Five freely selectable original environments with day/sunset/night and clear/rain/snow settings, shared by the habitat and Touch Bar.
- A dedicated Paw World panel, owned decorations with explicit game-coin purchases and free re-equipping.
- Three ordered permanent adventures, seven earned badges, lifetime statistics and once-only chapter rewards.
- Fetch Dash: a 30-second aim-and-return challenge using the actual moving pet, gold target zones, completion-only scoring and a saved personal best.
- Challenge pause and persistence retry retain movement, timing and already-completed returns without duplicate rewards.
- Automatic version-1 to version-2 save migration preserves existing pet progress; new counters start at zero. Older apps cannot read the new archive format.
- Expanded core/AppKit migration, purchase, chapter, challenge and retry verification, plus actual five-world animated previews.

## 1.1.0 · 2026-10-05

- A living, shared playground: autonomous walks, gradual touch following, running, turning, blinking and articulated paws/tail.
- Ball and bone throwing with flight, chase, pickup, carry-back and celebration phases.
- Place a food bowl anywhere; the pet walks over and eats before needs and progress are saved.
- Animated cuddle/wash, a dedicated physical Touch Bar toy menu and matching window/habitat controls.
- Completion-only care settlement, cancellation without rewards, failed-save retry and unchanged version-1 save compatibility.
- Reduce Motion disables decorative animation and autonomous roaming while explicit interaction remains usable.
- An animated preview rendered from actual native Touch Bar views, plus expanded motion and AppKit verification.

## 1.0.0 · 2026-10-05

- Adopt a named cat, dog or rabbit with three fur colors and level-unlocked accessories.
- Free feeding, washing, cuddling and persistent resting; optional paw-coin treat.
- Three shared Touch Bar/window games: Star Hunt, Ball Rally and Paw Pattern.
- Transparent daily trio, bounded care rewards and once-per-round settlement.
- Atomic local progress, previous-revision recovery and validated JSON backups.
- Turkish/English controls and original native vector art.
- Universal macOS 11+ delivery, native Intel/arm64 CI and packaged-app acceptance.
